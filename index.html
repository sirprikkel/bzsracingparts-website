About B.Z.S.

Crafted with Precision

With great precision B.Z.S. Racing Parts builds motorsport frames for cross (MX), racing and quad. All frames are constructed from chromium molybdenum tube and plate of the same tensile strength. Our frames are MIG or TIG welded using special welding wire of the same composition as the tube.

All frames are mechanically finished after welding and checked on several quality aspects. The bending work is done by means of a computer-controlled 3-axes MACRI bending machine, which stands for high accuracy.
